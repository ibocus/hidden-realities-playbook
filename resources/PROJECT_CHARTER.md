| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| Capture workflow feels burdensome | Keep it 5-10 min per story; rough notes OK; monthly syncs for polish |
| LinkedIn audience doesn't grow | Adjust post frequency, engage more on others' posts, join groups, ask network to share |

| Stories feel "too generic" after anonymization | Keep original workbook raw; anonymize only for public posts |
| Unclear when to draft chapters | Monthly review will flag when we have 15+ stories for a theme (ready to draft) |

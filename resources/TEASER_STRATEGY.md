# Hidden Realities Playbook — Teaser Strategy & Audience Building

## THE CHALLENGE

Most "implementation guides" are generic theory. Your playbook is **real stories from an actual project**. That's the hook. But nobody knows it exists yet.

**Goal**: Build an audience of practitioners who recognize themselves in your stories *before* the playbook launches.

---

## PART 1: DO WE NEED A WEBSITE?

### Short Answer: Not Yet (Maybe Ever)

**Why not immediately:**
- Websites need traffic, and you don't have a reader base yet
- A website costs time/money and splits your effort
- LinkedIn is already where your audience lives (security leads, compliance officers, project leads)

**Better approach:**
- Build audience on LinkedIn first (free, built-in network, discovery)
- Use LinkedIn as your "distribution engine" for teaser content
- Website comes later (launch day or post-launch) when you have 500+ engaged followers and can drive them somewhere

### If You Want a Lightweight Website Later:
- Simple landing page: title, 2–3 teaser snippets, email signup, "Launching [Month]"
- Can be built in 4 hours with Carrd, Webflow, or even a Google Site
- Use email capture to build a waitlist (low-friction)

**For now: Skip website. Focus on LinkedIn.**

## PART 2: LINKEDIN TEASER STRATEGY

### Timeline & Goals

**Months 1–2 (Sept–Oct 2026)**: Build Foundation
- Post 1 teaser per week (4 posts total)
- Introduce the playbook concept, audience, pain point
- Position yourself as someone who's done this
- Goal: 100–200 followers engaged with playbook content

**Months 3–4 (Nov–Dec 2026)**: Story-Driven Teasers
- Post 2 per week from your capture workbook
- Real stories + lesson (not fully written, just the insight)
- Build curiosity: "Here's the hidden reality nobody talks about"
- Goal: 400+ followers, 5–10% engagement rate

**Month 5 (Jan 2027)**: Launch Prep
- 1 big announcement post
- "Playbook launching [specific date]"
- Where to buy/access
- Early-bird discount or exclusive pre-order

### The Teaser Post Formula

Each post should:
1. **Hook** (1–2 sentences): The false assumption
2. **Reality** (2–3 sentences): What actually happened
3. **Lesson** (1–2 sentences): The takeaway
4. **CTA** (optional): "Follow for more real implementation stories"

**Length**: 150–200 words (short, punchy, scrollable)

**Tone**: Honest, slightly skeptical of standard guidance, practical

**Example**:

---

**Most ISO 27001 guides tell you to map controls and document them.**

I believed that too, until we hit our first discovery workshop.

We assumed our 200-person org had *most* processes documented. Seemed reasonable—they've been around 15 years.

Reality? 40% of critical controls lived nowhere but in people's heads. Spreadsheets, ad-hoc notes, tribal knowledge. No formal procedures.

That discovery took 3 weeks and threw our timeline sideways. We had to stop mapping and start *documenting first*.

**Lesson**: Before you assess your gaps against ISO, you need to know what you actually *have* documented. A discovery audit of existing docs is Step Zero, not Step Three.

This is one of 47 hidden realities we captured during our ISMS implementation. More coming.

## PART 7: SALES & DISTRIBUTION

### Option A: Self-Publish Digital (Simplest)
- PDF or ePub (easy to create, mail to buyers)
- Host on Gumroad, SendOwl, or Payhip ($10–20/month)
- Price: $29–49
- Effort: Low. Profits: 90%+

### Option B: Amazon KDP (Wider Reach)
- Print-on-demand + Kindle
- Reach more people, but Amazon takes 45–55%
- Less profitable, but more visibility
- Effort: Medium (formatting)

### Option C: Direct Sales (Highest Profit, Least Reach)
- Sell directly via email/LinkedIn
- Send PDF after payment (PayPal, Stripe)
- Price: $39–59
- Effort: Low (process setup). Profits: 95%+

### Hybrid (Recommended for Start)
- Primary: Direct sales via email/LinkedIn (highest profit)
- Secondary: Gumroad (for discovery/convenience)
- Later: KDP if you want wider reach

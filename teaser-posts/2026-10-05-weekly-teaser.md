## LinkedIn Draft

I assumed that once our documentation set was written, reviewed, and signed off, the hard part was behind us. Policies approved, procedures filed, version numbers on the cover page. That felt like evidence enough.

It isn't. The real question that actually stopped us: how do you show an auditor, or honestly yourself, that this isn't just paper? Sitting with that, the answer was not yet. A version number proves someone edited a file. It doesn't prove the control described in it actually runs.

Three things came out of that discussion. Version history needs a real change log, who changed what and why, not just "v1.0 approved." KPIs only mean something as a trend across months, not a single snapshot ("100% of policies approved" proves nothing alone). And certification bodies expect roughly three months of operating history before Stage 2, so there's a real cycle of evidence to sample, not a system switched on the week before the audit.

That last point is the expensive lesson. The discussion took an hour. The real cost is however many months of tracked KPIs now need to fit into a timeline that never budgeted for them.

Closing in on Stage 2 yourself? Curious where your evidence trail stands. DM me, happy to compare notes.

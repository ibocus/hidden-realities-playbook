## LinkedIn Draft

Most people assume a book like this gets written after the project wraps up. Finish the implementation, look back over the whole thing, pull out the highlights, write it up from memory.

That's not how Hidden Realities in ISO 27001 Implementation is getting written. Every story goes down within days of it actually happening, while the friction is still fresh, before hindsight smooths it into something cleaner than it actually was.

The difference matters more than it sounds. Memory edits inconvenient details. It forgets how long a "simple" call like documentation language really took to settle, or how many people had to weigh in before a scope document got signed off. Capturing it in real time keeps the mess in, on purpose.

It's also why none of the stories so far have touched a single control. Real implementations don't start there, whatever the standard implies.

If you're mid-implementation right now, the friction you hit this week is worth writing down before it fades. What's the one you'd still want to remember in six months? Send me a DM, I'd genuinely like to hear it.

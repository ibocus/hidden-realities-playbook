## LinkedIn Draft

I used to think each "hidden reality" in this book was its own one-off surprise. Four stories in, that is not the pattern.

Every one of them has the same shape: a point where the standard goes quiet. It tells you to document, not what language to write in. It tells you what documents to produce, not where they should live or who gets to see the unapproved version. It tells you to get sign-off, not that the sponsor's own understanding has to match the paper. It tells you to produce evidence, not that evidence only counts once it is a trend across months.

Four different topics. One identical gap. The standard names the output and stays silent on the judgment call sitting right next to it.

That is not a flaw in ISO 27001. It is just not what the standard is for. But if you are implementing it expecting the text to flag these calls for you, you will hit every one of them as a surprise, the same way we did.

Which standard-shaped silence are you navigating right now? Tell me in a DM, I want to hear it.

## LinkedIn Draft

Most people picture a "playbook" as a few hundred pages you read once, nod along to, and shelve.

That's not what's taking shape here. Alongside the stories, the project has been generating a small set of working templates: a document register that now requires a real change summary on every revision, not just a version bump. A monthly KPI tracker tied to the actual risk treatment plan instead of vanity metrics like "policies approved." And the Draft/Final folder split that decides what an auditor is even allowed to see.

None of these came from a template library. They came out of getting stuck on a specific question mid-implementation and building the smallest thing that actually answered it.

The lesson: a toolkit earns its place by getting reused every week, not by looking complete on page one. If a template doesn't survive contact with a messy real project, it doesn't belong in the book.

What's actually in your own toolkit right now, the stuff you built because nothing off-the-shelf fit? Drop it in a comment or send me a DM, I'm deciding what makes the book's appendix.

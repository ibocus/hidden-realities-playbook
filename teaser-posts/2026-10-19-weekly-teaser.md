## LinkedIn Draft

When this project started, I assumed the friction would all look the same: speed bumps you clear once near the beginning, then smooth sailing into the technical work.

Four stories into capturing this book in real time, that assumption doesn't hold. The first three (what language to document in, where files live and who can see drafts, getting the scope document actually signed) all landed inside the first ten days. Different topics, same shape: decide this before you touch a single control, decide it once, move on.

The fourth story showed up six weeks later, after the documentation set was already written, reviewed, and approved. It wasn't a setup decision at all. It was the harder question of whether an approved document actually proves a control runs, or just proves someone edited a file. That friction doesn't resolve in a meeting. It resolves over months of tracked evidence.

Standard guidance doesn't flag that the nature of the friction changes as a project matures. Early pain is structural and clears fast. Later pain is about credibility, and that does not clear fast at all.

If you're mid-implementation, which kind are you hitting right now? The one-time setup kind, or the slow credibility kind? DM me, I'd like to know.
